FreeFlow

Showing sample data (synthetic). Opening your own files replaces it.

Analyse flow cytometry data in your browser

Open FCS files to gate, compensate and build figures. Your files stay on your computer: nothing is uploaded.

You can also drop files or a folder anywhere on this page.

Folders

    A sample can be in several folders. Drag a sample onto a folder to add it; drag a gate onto a folder to copy it to every sample in it.

    Samples

      Samples are in the order they were run; type a number in a box to change the order in this folder. Ctrl/⌘- or Shift-click to select several, then drag them to a folder or press Delete. Drag gates onto any population to copy them there.

      Populations

        Ctrl/⌘-click or Shift-click to select several gates, then drag them together. Esc clears the selection.

        Click a tile to show that sample above. Drag a gate in a tile to change that sample only.

        Statistics

        Samples

          The samples of the folder picked above, in its order and with their names. Click a population to add its plot to the page, or drag it to where you want it.

          Draw, move and reshape gates here; the figure updates as you go. Double-click a gate to rename it; Delete removes the selected gate. Esc or Done closes.
