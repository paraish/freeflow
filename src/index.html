FreeFlow

Showing sample data (synthetic). Opening your own files replaces it.

Analyse flow cytometry data in your browser

Open FCS files to gate, compensate and build figures. Your files stay on your computer: nothing is uploaded.

You can also drop files or a folder anywhere on this page.

Samples

    Drag gates onto any population in any sample to copy them there. Drop on a sample's name to copy them to the same place in its tree.

    Populations

      Ctrl/⌘-click or Shift-click to select several gates, then drag them together. Esc clears the selection.

            Just the gates, axis settings and compensation as a FlowJo 10 workspace (.wsp), for when the other person already has the FCS files. Uses the name above.
